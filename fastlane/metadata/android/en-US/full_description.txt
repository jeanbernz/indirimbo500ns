Indirimbo 500 ni igitabo cy'indirimbo zo guhimbaza Imana kirimo indirimbo 500 mu Kinyarwanda. App iracyagizwa neza kugira ngo abakoresha babone indirimbo byoroshye no gusenga hamwe nabo babikunda.

🎵 IBIRIMO / FEATURES:

✅ Indirimbo 500 zose
Indirimbo zose 500 zo guhimbaza Imana zanditswe neza kandi ziraboneka ari offline — nta internet igire.

✅ Shakiro ryoroshye
Shakisha indirimbo ukoresheje inomero cyangwa ukoresheje amagambo ya mbere y'indirimbo.

✅ Pad ya nimero
Injiza nomero y'indirimbo mwihuse ukoresheje pad ya nimero isa na telephone — buraruka, yoroshye.

✅ Fonti ikuza
Hindura ingano y'inyuguti ukurikije uko ushaka — ni byiza cyane kuri ba sekuru n'abakuze.

✅ Moderi ya nijoro
Soma uteye neza mu gikorwa cy'ijoro — amaso yawe ntazaruha.

✅ Sangiza inshuti
Sangiza indirimbo n'inshuti n'umuryango wawe vuba muri WhatsApp, SMS na sosiyete media.

✅ Ifite ISHAKIRO
Reba ishakiro ryose ry'indirimbo kandi ubumenye aho biri byoroshye.

✅ Ntabwo isaba internet
Ikorera neza ari offline — uyifungura aho uri hose, igihe cyose.
